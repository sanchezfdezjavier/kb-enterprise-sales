## 1. Founder-Led Sales Is A PMF Learning System

Repeated pattern: founders should not delegate sales before the company has learned the market. Early sales is less about revenue and more about eliminating wrong assumptions, learning language, and finding the wedge.

## 2. Enterprise Sales Rewards Insider Context

Repeated pattern: the best sellers collect internal pressure, gossip, budget logic, and buyer-side politics before trying to close. A deal becomes real when the buyer shares context that competitors do not have.

## 3. Demos And Pilots Are Often Too Early

Repeated pattern: generic demos, group demos, and early pilots flatten the sale. Discovery and context should come first. Pilots belong late in the process.

## 4. Pricing Requires Budget And Defense Context

Repeated pattern: do not quote pricing before understanding how the buyer will fund and defend the purchase internally. Enterprise deals are about perceived value, internal category, and buyer confidence.
