Source: [$1M to $10M: The enterprise sales playbook with Jen Abel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37fKFWdrMyA)

Focus: moving from founder-led traction into enterprise sales that can scale from $1M to $10M ARR.

## Core Thesis

The $1M to $10M stage is a shift from proving that anyone will buy to learning how enterprise buyers actually create urgency, defend budget, and circulate a story internally. The founder still matters, but the company now needs a motion that can preserve founder-level vision while adding repeatability.

## Usable Takeaways

- Do not drift into an undefined mid-market motion. SMB and enterprise are different games.
- Tier-one logos can be better early adopters because they want differentiated edge and can co-create.
- Enterprise buyers need an internal story: why change, why now, why this vendor.
- Pricing too low can put the product into a dead zone where it is too expensive for self-serve and too small to justify enterprise attention.
- Services can create a path into the enterprise when the product alone is not yet enough.
- Design partners are useful for learning, but converting them into full rollout customers is hard.
- The first sales hire should be able to "cosplay" the founder: carry vision, improvise with context, and pattern-match subtle signals.
