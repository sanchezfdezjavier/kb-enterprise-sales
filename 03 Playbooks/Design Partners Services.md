# Design Partners Services

## Design Partners

Design partners are useful for learning, credibility, and product shaping, but Jen repeatedly warns that they can be difficult to convert into full rollout customers.

Why they are hard:

- They often entered under a special learning frame.
- They may not have committed real buying pressure.
- They may have helped shape the product but not the budget narrative.
- They can blur feedback, custom work, and purchase intent.

## How To Use Them Well

- Be clear about the learning objective.
- Time-box the relationship.
- Track whether the buyer can articulate a real internal case.
- Do not count design partner enthusiasm as expansion proof.
- Separate product learning from sales qualification.

## Services As A Wedge

Services can be a fast way into the enterprise when the product is early or the market needs education. The service should not become random labor. It should create:

- Trust.
- Workflow access.
- Insider context.
- A clearer path to the productized wedge.
- A buyer-owned story for why the full solution matters.

## Risk

Services can become a distraction if they are not time-boxed and tied to a repeatable product insight or expansion path.
