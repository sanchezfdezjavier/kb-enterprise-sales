## High-Signal Themes Seen In The Capture

- Founder-led sales is a learning engine before it is a revenue engine.
- Enterprise selling is mostly insider context, buying-process project management, and narrative co-authoring.
- Early enterprise buyers want a differentiated edge, not a complete generic solution.
- Pilots, demos, pricing, and procurement happen late, after trust and internal context are built.
- The best first sales hire can carry founder-level vision, pattern-match messy context, and avoid over-polished sales behavior.
- Strong relationship signals include cell/text access, off-the-record context, internal pressure, and buyer-owned language.
