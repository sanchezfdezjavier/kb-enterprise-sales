# Founder-Led Sales

## Definition

Founder-led sales is the phase where the founder directly sells, discovers, qualifies, and learns from the market before the company has earned the right to delegate sales.

## Jen Abel's View

In the earliest stage, the founder is part of what the buyer is buying. The buyer is not only evaluating software; they are evaluating the founder's vision, judgment, urgency, taste, and ability to turn ambiguity into a useful outcome.

## Why It Matters

- The founder has the clearest view of the product's future.
- The founder can hear weak signals that a hired seller would miss.
- The founder can adjust product, positioning, ICP, and story in one loop.
- Buyers often give more honest feedback when the founder is open about the product still being early.

## Common Mistake

Delegating sales too early. Jen repeats this idea often: many startups miss product-market fit because the founder hands sales to a non-founder before the learning loop is mature.

## Practical Rule

Use early sales calls to test, eliminate, and refine. Revenue is useful, but it is a lagging indicator. The faster signal is whether the same kind of buyer keeps reacting to the same kind of problem, language, and wedge.
