## North Star

Jen's enterprise sales worldview is not "be better at pitching." It is closer to:

1. Learn faster than the market.
2. Earn insider context.
3. Co-author the buyer's internal story.
4. Project-manage the buying process.
5. Keep the founder's vision in the sale until the company has earned the right to delegate it.
